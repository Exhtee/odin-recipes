Red Lentil Curry

Description

Recipe for the preparation of Red Lentil Curry.
Preparation time: 50 minutes.
Original recipe by: Emma Maher
The original recipe is obtained from: AllRecipes

Ingredients

  • 3 cups water, or more as needed
  • 2 cups red lentils
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 2 tablespoons curry paste
  • 1 tablespoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon minced fresh ginger
  • 1 (14.25 ounce) can tomato puree

Preparation Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Wash lentils in cold water until water runs clear.
  3. Put lentils in pot and cover with water.
  4. Heat lentils until water boils, then reduce heat to medium-low.
  5. Cover pot & simmer, adding water as needed to keep lentils immersed.
  6. Stop cooking once lentil is tender (approx. 15 to 20 minutes).
  7. Drain lentils.
  8. Heat vegetable oil in large skillet over medium heat
  9. Cook & stir onions in hot oil until caramelised, (~20 min)
  10. In a large bowl, mix together:
    • Curry paste
    • Curry
    • Powder
    • Turmeric
    • Cumin
    • Chili
    • Powder
    • Salt
    • Sugar
    • Garlic
    • Ginger
  11. Stir the above mixture into the caramelised onions
  12. Increase heat to high and cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes.
  13. Stir in tomato puree & lentils; cook on medium heat for another 5 to 10 min.
  14. Serve and enjoy.
