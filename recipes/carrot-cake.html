Carrot Cake

Description

Recipe for the preparation of Carrot Cake with optional cream cheese frosting
Preparation time: 50 minutes
Recipe by: Allrecipes Community
Original recipe from: AllRecipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups white sugar
  • 1 ¼ cups vegetable oil
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 3 cups grated carrots
  • 1 cup chopped pecans

Optional: Frosting ingredients

  • 4 cups confectioners' sugar
  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 175 degrees C. While waiting gather all ingredients.
  2. Grease & flour a 23x33 cm pan.
  3. Beat white sugar, oil, eggs, & 2 teaspoons vanilla together in a large bowl.
  4. Mix in flour, bakig soda, baking powder, cinnamon, & salt.
  5. Stir in carrots and.1 cup of pecans.
  6. Pour into the prepared pan.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven for about 40 minutes.
  8. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool.
  9. Serve and enjoy without frosting, or continue to prepare frosting.

Optional: Frosting

  1. Beat confectioner's sugar, cream cheese, butter & 1 teaspoon of vanilla togehter in a large bowl.
  2. Beat until smooth and creamy.
  3. Stire in 1 cup of chopped pecans
  4. Frost the cooled cake.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
