Chickpea Curry

Description

Recipe for the preparation of chickpea curry made using canned chickpeas.
Preparation time: 40 minutes
Recipe by: Aminah Rahman
Original recipe from: AllRecipes

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 onions, minced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoons fresh ginger root, finely chopped
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 2 (2-inch) sticks cinnamon, crushed
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • pinch of salt
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 2 (425g) cans chickpeas, with liquid
  • 1 cup chopped fresh cilantro

Preparation Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. In a large frying pan, heat oil over medium heat.
  3. Add onions to frying pan, cook & stir until tender (3 to 5 minutes).
  4. Stir in garlic, ginger, cloves, cinnamon, cumin, coriander, salt, cayenne & tumeric.
  5. Cook for 1 minute over medium heat with constant stirring.
  6. Mix in the chickpeas with the liquid and continue to cook.
  7. Remove frying pan from heat and stir in cilantro just before serving
  8. Serve and enjoy!
